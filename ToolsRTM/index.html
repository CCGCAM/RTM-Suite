ToolsRTM · Radiative Transfer Package

ToolsRTM provides a unified interface to multiple radiative transfer models (RTMs) for simulating leaf, canopy, soil and top-of-atmosphere optical signals. The package is used within the ESA HYDRA-EO project to generate spectral libraries, trait simulations and forward-modelling experiments for hyperspectral, multispectral, fluorescence and thermal Earth Observation.

Overview

ToolsRTM integrates multiple radiative transfer models under a common R interface. Leaf optical RTMs, canopy reflectance models, soil RTMs and soil–plant–atmosphere couplings can be accessed via get.simulator(). The package supports trait retrieval, LUT generation, forward modelling and EO mission analysis within HYDRA-EO.

Leaf & canopy

Core RTMs

Access to leaf and canopy models such as PROSPECT, PROSAIL, FLUSPECT, fourSAIL, fourSAIL2 and INFORM, enabling multi-scale simulation of vegetation optical properties.

Soil & TOA

MARMIT & SPART

Soil radiative transfer (MARMIT) and soil–plant–atmosphere TOA simulations (SPART) support multi-sensor modelling and background correction for agricultural and natural targets.

Integration

SCOPEinR & Shiny

ToolsRTM is designed to work together with SCOPEinR for fluorescence and energy-balance simulations and includes a Shiny-based interface for interactive model exploration (as described in the ToolsRTM documentation).

Basic PROSAIL simulation (HYDRA-EO example)

This minimal example (used in the HYDRA-EO web page) illustrates how to obtain a PROSAIL simulator and plot a reflectance spectrum.

Show PROSAIL code ▾ 

library(ToolsRTM)

sim <- ToolsRTM::get.simulator(app = "PROSAIL")
out <- sim(Cab = 45, LAI = 3.2)

plot(out$wl, out$R, type = "l",
     xlab = "Wavelength (nm)",
     ylab = "Reflectance")

Scientific references

Key scientific references underpinning the radiative transfer models accessed via ToolsRTM.

Leaf optical models

Jacquemoud, S., & Baret, F. (1990). PROSPECT: A model of leaf optical properties.
Vilfan, N., et al. (2016, 2018). FLUSPECT-B and FLUSPECT-Cx for modelling chlorophyll fluorescence emission and leaf optical properties.

Canopy reflectance models

Verhoef, W. (1984–1998). SAIL family of canopy reflectance models.
Atzberger, C. (2000). INFORM: A canopy reflectance model integrating leaf optics, canopy structure and background effects.

Soil radiative transfer

MARMIT-1 and MARMIT-2 soil radiative transfer models calibrated against multiple soil spectral datasets (Bablet-2016, Dupiau-2020, Humper-2015, Lesaignoux-2008, Liu-2002, Lobell-2002, Marcq-2012, Philpot-2014).

Soil–plant–atmosphere (SPART)

SPART: Coupled soil BRDF, PROSAIL and atmospheric radiative transfer for directional top-of-atmosphere reflectance and EO sensor simulation.

SCOPE (energy balance and fluorescence)

Van der Tol, C., et al. (2009). SCOPE: A model to simulate energy balance, fluorescence and reflectance of a vegetated surface.
Yang, P., et al. (2020). Updates and extensions to the SCOPE model for improved canopy energy-balance and SIF simulations.
